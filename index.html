Privacy

        
      

      

        

          Your receipts never leave your device.

          Receipt photos are processed entirely in your browser using on-device OCR (Tesseract.js).
          No image or text is uploaded to any server.
        

        

          Everything stays local.

          Bill items, people names, assignments, and preferences are stored only in your browser's
          local storage. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
        

        

          No cookies, no ads.

          This page is a static tool. It does not set tracking cookies or serve advertisements.

The goal was simplicity. No accounts, no backend, no bloat — just a tool that solves a daily use case: splitting a bill without the awkward math.
